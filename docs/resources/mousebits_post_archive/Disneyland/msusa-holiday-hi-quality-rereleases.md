# Main Street U.S.A. Holiday High Quality Track Rereleases

Over the years, many of the MSUSA holiday loop tracks have been re-released in high quality, such that we can successfully reconstruct the loop today (almost) without a single transfer from Al Lutz’s original post. He has also updated his post as of 2020: [https://www.micechat.com/278726-holiday-special-disneylands-main-street-music-loop/](https://www.micechat.com/278726-holiday-special-disneylands-main-street-music-loop/)

My notes on sourcing these tracks are below. Many thanks to VJ and IHeartGaming for their work in finding many of these.
