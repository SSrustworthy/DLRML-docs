# Big Thunder Mountain Railroad Queue

**Land(s)**: Frontierland

**Loop Total Length**: 00:19:09

**Dates**: September 2, 1979

This loop plays in the staircase and loading dock of the Big Thunder Mountain Railroad queue from its opening in 1979 through present day. Due to its isolated location, no complete reference recording appears to exist. Horizons notes that this is the same loop that played at Magic Kingdom when its version of the attration first opened.  At some point in the late 1980s or early 1990s, one additional track was added to the Magic Kingdom version ("Home, Sweet Home”). Tracklist originally posted by Progressland on Magic Music, [https://community.magicmusic.net/threads/disneyland-music-loops.17/post-72](https://community.magicmusic.net/threads/disneyland-music-loops.17/post-72) (2002).
