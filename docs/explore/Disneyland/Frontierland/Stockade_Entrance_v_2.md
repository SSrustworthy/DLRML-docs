# Stockade Entrance v.2

**Land(s)**: Frontierland

**Loop Total Length**: ~00:06:00

**Dates**: January 1, 1992 → January 1, 2012

This loop played at the entrance to the Stockade starting around 1992 until 2012. There are conflicting reports on this loop from Magic Music, [https://community.magicmusic.net/threads/disneyland-music-loops.17/post-692](https://community.magicmusic.net/threads/disneyland-music-loops.17/post-692)  and MouseBits, [https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/blockade-house-battle-cry-of-freedom.7393/](https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/blockade-house-battle-cry-of-freedom.7393/) , but the loop tracklist was obtained from a Disney lawyer after a misattribution error with Ed Sweeney’s “Battle Cry of Freedom” which was released on A Musical History of Disneyland box set. That updated tracklist is listed here. SSrustworthy has written up extensive notes on this loop on MouseBits, Re: [https://mousebits.com/smf/index.php?topic=10822.msg98895#msg98895](https://mousebits.com/smf/index.php?topic=10822.msg98895#msg98895)  .
