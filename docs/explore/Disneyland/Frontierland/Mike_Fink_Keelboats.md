# Mike Fink Keelboats

**Land(s)**: Frontierland

**Loop Total Length**: ~00:07:00

**Dates**: April 12, 1992 → April 29, 2001

These songs played on the Keelboats while in motion on the Rivers of America. See MouseBits, [https://mousebits.com/smf/index.php?topic=7514.msg98970#msg98970](https://mousebits.com/smf/index.php?topic=7514.msg98970#msg98970)  and Magic Music, [https://community.magicmusic.net/threads/disneylands-mike-fink-keel-boats.4963/.](https://community.magicmusic.net/threads/disneylands-mike-fink-keel-boats.4963/.)  Three tracks were said to have played by DL FLIGHT 295, see Magic Music, [https://community.magicmusic.net/threads/frontierland-loop-question.3677/post-25758.](https://community.magicmusic.net/threads/frontierland-loop-question.3677/post-25758.)  These tracks were released on various records by the Banjo Kings. The track “Down South” is the only one known to be released to CD on “The Banjo Kings, Vol. 1” which is also on streaming platforms.
