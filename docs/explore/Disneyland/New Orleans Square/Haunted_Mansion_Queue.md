# Haunted Mansion Queue

**Land(s)**: New Orleans Square

**Loop Total Length**: ~10:06

**Dates**: November 21, 2024

This custom queue loop began playing in the exterior garden area with the reopening of the Haunted Mansion in January 2025. Variations on a central track play in sync across the 4 different areas of the queue, which includes the Statue Courtyard, Madame Leota’s Sanctum Garden, the Mansion Greenhouse, and the Pet & Residential Cemeteries (ghostly choral music). The music was composed by [https://www.egoplum.com/.](https://www.egoplum.com/.)  A sample of his work for the queue music is [https://soundcloud.com/laughing-place/haunted-mansion-new-queue-area-music-disneyland.](https://soundcloud.com/laughing-place/haunted-mansion-new-queue-area-music-disneyland.)  See MouseBits, [https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/new-haunted-mansion-queue-music.11081/](https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/new-haunted-mansion-queue-music.11081/)  (2025). A reference recording is available on [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vSxRIbxLRSo&list=RDvSxRIbxLRSo&start_radio=1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vSxRIbxLRSo&list=RDvSxRIbxLRSo&start_radio=1)  .
