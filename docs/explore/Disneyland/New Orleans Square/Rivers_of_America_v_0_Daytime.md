# Rivers of America v.0 Daytime

**Land(s)**: Frontierland, New Orleans Square

**Loop Total Length**: 00:58:42

This loop played for a short time ca. 1994, but prior to 1996, along the riverfront. It is labeled as “Day” and so thought to have played in the daytime. These loops were first mentioned by Magic Music forums user Progressland (Jason Schultz), who received them from correspondence with Eddie Sotto prior to his forum post in 2002, [https://community.magicmusic.net/threads/disneyland-music-loops.17/page-2#post-218.](https://community.magicmusic.net/threads/disneyland-music-loops.17/page-2#post-218.)  SSrustworthy confirmed with Eddie Sotto via personal correspondence that this loop and its nighttime equivalent played in the parks for some time, see MouseBits, [https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/eddie-sotto-recent-parks-bgm-correspondence-in-memoriam.11197/](https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/eddie-sotto-recent-parks-bgm-correspondence-in-memoriam.11197/)  (2025). The source loop files were posted with tracklists on MouseBits, [https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/nos-1994-night-and-day-loops-unverified.10878/](https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/nos-1994-night-and-day-loops-unverified.10878/)  (2023). The pair of day/night loops share several tracks.
