# Peter Pan's Flight Queue

**Land(s)**: Fantasyland

**Loop Total Length**: 00:09:16

**Dates**: May 25, 1983

This loop plays in the Peter Pan’s Flight covered queue area. All tracks are instrumental versions of songs or score from the animated film; however they are not 1:1 with the released soundtrack and likely come from the international masters if the sountrack (ready to be dubbed in different languages). This includes “You Can Fly!”, “Following the Leader”, and “A Pirate’s Life”. A tracklist was originally posted 03/2002 by Progressland on Magic Music of the Mouse discussion board, but this was inaccurate due to the amount of score cues that were not released. See Magic Music, [https://community.magicmusic.net/threads/disneyland-music-loops.17/](https://community.magicmusic.net/threads/disneyland-music-loops.17/)  and Disney Parks Music Compendium [https://disney-parks-music-compendium.blogspot.com/2022/11/peter-pans-flight-queue-bgm-1983.html](https://disney-parks-music-compendium.blogspot.com/2022/11/peter-pans-flight-queue-bgm-1983.html)  .
