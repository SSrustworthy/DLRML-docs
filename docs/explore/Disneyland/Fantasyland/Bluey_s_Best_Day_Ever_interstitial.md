# Bluey's Best Day Ever! interstitial

**Land(s)**: Fantasyland

**Loop Total Length**: 57:31.536

This is a collection of music that plays between showings of Bluey’s Best Day Ever at the Fantasyland Theatre, which debuted March 22, 2026. Loop length identified based on reference recordings by pixelated. All tracks appear to be underlying scene music from the Bluey animated series that are unreleased. Between two shows, a 5-track run of released Bluey score (Omelette -> Pool -> Fairytale -> Cafe -> Grandad) may be played while the BGM is faded to inaudible levels, but this is not a part of the actual BGM loop. The tracks Omelette and Pool are always played at the conclusion of the show.
