# King Arthur Carrousel v.1 [PRE]

**Land(s)**: Fantasyland

**Loop Total Length**: Unknown

This tracklist is presumed to have been the first recorded band organ music for King Arthur Carrousel. Tracklist is from the Jack Wagner estate for the Fantasyland Carrousel, intended for both Disneyland and Walt Disney World, dated January 1973. A note on the tracklist reads: “Recorded from custom-made rolls played on the Disneyland Carrousel. Recording Rights paid by Walt Disney Productions to TRT Manufacturing Co. 138 Miller St., North Tonowanda, N.Y.,14120. Ralph Tussing, Owner. (Deceased).”
