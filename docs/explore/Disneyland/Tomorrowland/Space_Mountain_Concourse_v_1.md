# Space Mountain Concourse v.1

**Land(s)**: Tomorrowland

**Loop Total Length**: 00:24:06

**Dates**: October 1, 1977 → September 1, 1986

This track is a collection of synthesized moog music composed by Don Dorsey that is thought to have played around the Space Mountain entrance/Space Stage area from October 1977 to around September 1986 (when Space Stage was enclosed into the Magic Eye Theater/Captain EO and the augmented EPCOT Center entrance loop was brought in). A source track is in general circulation, but there is no live reference recording. See MouseBits, [https://mousebits.com/smf/index.php?topic=7450.msg64774#msg64774](https://mousebits.com/smf/index.php?topic=7450.msg64774#msg64774)  (2012), [https://community.magicmusic.net/threads/space-mountain-music-question.641/#post-4158](https://community.magicmusic.net/threads/space-mountain-music-question.641/#post-4158)  (2003) and [https://mousebits.com/smf/index.php?topic=9854.msg98981#msg98981](https://mousebits.com/smf/index.php?topic=9854.msg98981#msg98981)  (2022).
