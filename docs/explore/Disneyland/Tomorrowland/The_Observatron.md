# The Observatron

**Land(s)**: Tomorrowland

**Loop Total Length**: N/A

**Dates**: May 22, 1998

An edited version of one of these tracks played every 15 minutes from the Observatron was it was in motion. It is not a true “loop” per se. Discussed on MouseBits: [https://mousebits.com/smf/index.php?topic=7942.msg79423#msg79423.](https://mousebits.com/smf/index.php?topic=7942.msg79423#msg79423.)  The observatron was introduced with Tomorrowland in 1998, and its last working operation was sometime between 2013 and 2017.
