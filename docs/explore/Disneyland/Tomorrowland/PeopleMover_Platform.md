# PeopleMover Platform

**Land(s)**: Tomorrowland

**Loop Total Length**: Unknown

**Dates**: July 2, 1967 → August 21, 1995

This loop played on the PeopleMover Goodyear speedramp (inclined, moving sidewalk) to transport guests to the elevated loading platform. Discussed by Mike Cozart. See Magic Music, [https://community.magicmusic.net/threads/go-go-goodyear-ge-and-ford.458/#post-2881](https://community.magicmusic.net/threads/go-go-goodyear-ge-and-ford.458/#post-2881)  (2002) and [https://community.magicmusic.net/threads/frontierland-shooting-gallery-bgm.865/post-6139](https://community.magicmusic.net/threads/frontierland-shooting-gallery-bgm.865/post-6139)  (2003).
