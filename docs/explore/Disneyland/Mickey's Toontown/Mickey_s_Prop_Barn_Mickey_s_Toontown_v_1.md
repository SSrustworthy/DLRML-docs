# Mickey's Prop Barn, Mickey's Toontown v.1

**Land(s)**: Mickey's Toontown

**Loop Total Length**: 01:00:40

**Dates**: January 24, 1993

This loop plays in the general Mickey’s Toontown proper, and in a small area of Mickey’s House known as Mickey’s Prop Barn (entered after the garden). Posted to [https://disneymusicloops.tripod.com/dlr/dlr-tt-tt.html](https://disneymusicloops.tripod.com/dlr/dlr-tt-tt.html)  . Discussed on Magic Music: [https://community.magicmusic.net/threads/mickeys-toontown-fair-area-music.3697/#post-36840](https://community.magicmusic.net/threads/mickeys-toontown-fair-area-music.3697/#post-36840)  and in the [https://archive.org/details/the-disneyland-forever-project.](https://archive.org/details/the-disneyland-forever-project.)  Also see MouseBits, [https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/mickeys-toontown-v-1-tracklist.11180/](https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/mickeys-toontown-v-1-tracklist.11180/)  (2025). These tracks were recorded in October 1992. Track notes and versions are based on a recording by pixelated, 2011.
