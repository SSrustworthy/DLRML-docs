# Swiss Family Robinson Treehouse

**Land(s)**: Adventureland

**Loop Total Length**: 00:01:52

**Dates**: November 18, 1962 → March 8, 1999

The Swisskapolka could be heard from the pump organ in the living room of the Swiss Family Robinson Treehouse. This can be labeled as “attraction music” that functioned as BGM for the larger area. The attraction operated from 1962-1999.
