# High Key Club​

**Area**: Disneyland Hotel

**Loop Total Length**: 01:40:09

This loop plays inside the High Key Club, the new concierge lounge at the Disneyland Hotel Adventureland Tower (11th floor) which opened on July 10, 2025. Inspired by the music and sounds of Disneyland Park circa 1955, Disneyland Hotel club-level guests can enjoy food and beverage in this mid-century modern lounge that pays homage to the history of audible magic at the park. Tracklist compiled by elecTRON rabbit based on his August 2025 reference recording. elecTRONrabbit notes that the loop is inspired by the "Walt Disney Takes You to Disneyland" album from 1956, and features partial clips of Walt from that album alongside park SFX. These "Added Clips" that play between tracks are markedly louder than the music, sometimes overpowering conversations in the room. See MouseBits, [https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/high-key-club-disneyland-hotel.11181/](https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/high-key-club-disneyland-hotel.11181/)  (2025).
