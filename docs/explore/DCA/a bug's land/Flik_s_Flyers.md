# Flik's Flyers

**Land(s)**: "a bug's land"

**Loop Total Length**: 00:01:24.553 (with narration);
00:01:21.625 (music-only)

**Dates**: October 7, 2002 → September 4, 2018

This track played as onride music for the Flik’s Flyers attraction. It was introduced with the opening of Cars Land on June 13, 2012.  It is only audible during the ride duration. Track edits confirmed by SSrustworthy based on a reference recording by pixelated. See MouseBits, [https://mousebits.com/smf/index.php?topic=10829.msg98914#msg98914](https://mousebits.com/smf/index.php?topic=10829.msg98914#msg98914)  (2022).

## Tracklist

1\. The Flik Machine – Randy Newman – a bug’s life OST

- Track Notes: Edited; the attraction plays most of the latter half of the soundtrack version of Flik's Machine, starting from 00:01:25:685. The attraction version is only slightly edited, editing out a portion from ~2:40 to 2:44 of the album track.
