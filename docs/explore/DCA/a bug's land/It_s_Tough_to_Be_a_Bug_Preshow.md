# It's Tough to Be a Bug Preshow

**Land(s)**: "a bug's land"

**Loop Total Length**: ~00:09:08

**Dates**: October 7, 2002 → September 4, 2018

This loop played as a “preshow” in the waiting area of It’s Tough to Be a Bug! and played in the waiting area before the doors opened. This is thought to be the same preshow as the one at WDW’s Animal Kingdom. These custom compositions are renditions of popular tunes as performed by insects (humming and chirping). Tracklist compiled by SSrustworthy based on source audio in circulation. See MouseBits, [https://mousebits.com/smf/index.php?topic=10829.msg98914#msg98914](https://mousebits.com/smf/index.php?topic=10829.msg98914#msg98914)  .
