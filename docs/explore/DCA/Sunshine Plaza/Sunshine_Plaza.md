# Sunshine Plaza [REF]

**Land(s)**: Sunshine Plaza

**Loop Total Length**: ~00:45:38.946

This loop played in Sunshine Plaza from opening day (Feb 8, 2001) until its closure on August 29, 2011. The loop played in stereophonic sound, with one channel playing from the inner ring of speakers around the central hub, and the other channel playing from the outer ring of speakers across the parade route. It is rumored to have played in the Magic Key holder exclusive Magic Key Terrace at DCA (see [https://mousebits.com/smf/index.php?topic=10744.msg98404#msg98404](https://mousebits.com/smf/index.php?topic=10744.msg98404#msg98404)  (2022)). This tracklist has been verified by SSrustworthy against a reference recording by pixelated. Originally posted 03/2002 by Progressland on Magic Music, see [https://community.magicmusic.net/threads/disneyland-music-loops.17/post-168](https://community.magicmusic.net/threads/disneyland-music-loops.17/post-168)  (2002). Also see [http://web.archive.org/web/20041129022754/http://disneymusicloops.tripod.com/dlr/dlr-sp-sp.html](http://web.archive.org/web/20041129022754/http://disneymusicloops.tripod.com/dlr/dlr-sp-sp.html)  and [https://mousebits.com/smf/index.php?topic=4204.msg29209#msg29209](https://mousebits.com/smf/index.php?topic=4204.msg29209#msg29209)  (2024).
