# Coca Cola Gorilla

**Land(s)**: Hollywood Pictures Backlot

**Loop Total Length**: Unknown

This loop played in the vicinity of the Coca Cola Ice Cold Refreshment drive-in, where a gorilla in a convertible could be seen driving off with a trunkful of Coca Cola bottles. Track identified by SSrustworthy based on home footage by MouseBits user CThaddeus. See MouseBits, [https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/coca-cola-ice-cold-refreshment-drive-in-gorilla.11067/](https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/coca-cola-ice-cold-refreshment-drive-in-gorilla.11067/)  2024.

## Tracklist

1\. Too Much Monkey Business – Chuck Berry

- Track Notes: This is the only song in the loop. It could be heard between radio announcements about the gorilla on the loose (see MouseBits post for transcript).
