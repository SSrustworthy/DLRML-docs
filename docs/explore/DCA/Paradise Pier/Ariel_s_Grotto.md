# Ariel's Grotto

**Land(s)**: Paradise Pier

**Loop Total Length**: ~30 minutes

**Dates**: December 20, 2002 → January 8, 2018

This loop played in the interior of Ariel’s Grotto, presumably from its opening in 2002 until its retheming in January 2018. Based on [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QTCUL1hBdVA](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QTCUL1hBdVA) , the music apparently have played the Mermaid’s Lagoon exterior music from Tokyo Disneysea, which is a ~30 minute unreleased loop of various motifs from the soundtrack of The Little Mermaid with a soft orchestra. See MouseBits, [https://mousebits.com/smf/index.php?topic=10926.msg99404#msg99404](https://mousebits.com/smf/index.php?topic=10926.msg99404#msg99404)  .
