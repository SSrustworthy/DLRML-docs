# Hollywood Land

**Land(s)**: Hollywood Land

**Loop Total Length**: 01:06:36.089

This loop plays in Hollywood Land along the main avenue and in the surrounding area. It was introduced in May 2012 and plays currently (as of 2026). The same loop, with one additional track, plays in WDW’s DHS Trolley Car Cafe. This is a verified tracklist compiled by pixelated, RocketRodsXPR and eyore, and SSrustworthy based on pixelated’s reference recording. Note, most all of the loop tracks run slower than their source tracks by a few to several tenths of a second. See MouseBits, [https://mousebits.com/smf/index.php?topic=7298.msg62937#msg62937](https://mousebits.com/smf/index.php?topic=7298.msg62937#msg62937)  (2012) and [https://mousebits.com/smf/index.php?topic=9299.msg87975#msg87975](https://mousebits.com/smf/index.php?topic=9299.msg87975#msg87975)  (2016).
