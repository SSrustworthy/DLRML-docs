# Disneyana v.1, Disneyana v.3, [2012-2013] Realms of Fantasy – Designs From the Happiest Kingdoms of Them All!

**Location**: Main Street U.S.A. (Disneyana), Main Street U.S.A. (storefront)

**Loop Total Length**: ~00:28:00

This loop played in the Disney Gallery on Main Street, U.S.A. The loop was the BGM for the exhibit ”Realms of Fantasy  – Designs From the Happiest Kingdoms of Them All!” which was featured from December 14, 2012 through June 24, 2013. Tracklist compiled by wedroy1923 based on a 2012 recording by pixelated, with updates by SSrustworthy. As of July 2013, the Disneyana store (replacing the Disney Gallery) continued to play the Realms of Fantasy exhibit loop. See MouseBits, [https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/the-disney-gallery-music-2-0.7840/post-69938](https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/the-disney-gallery-music-2-0.7840/post-69938)  (2013) and [https://disney-parks-music-compendium.blogspot.com/2022/11/the-disney-gallery-realms-of-fantasy-bgm.html](https://disney-parks-music-compendium.blogspot.com/2022/11/the-disney-gallery-realms-of-fantasy-bgm.html)  (2022). Also see MouseBits, [https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/disneyana-loop-on-main-street-u-s-a.10894/post-100280](https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/disneyana-loop-on-main-street-u-s-a.10894/post-100280)  (2025).
