# [2002] 100 Mickeys [INC]

**Location**: New Orleans Square

**Loop Total Length**: Unknown

This loop played at the Disney Gallery in New Orleans Square. It was in use for the duration of the One Hundred Mickeys exhibit in 2002 (artwork commissioned from Eric Robison to celebrate Walt Disney’s 100th birthday). No track list exists for this loop. See MouseBits, [https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/the-disney-gallery-music-2-0.7840/#post-73222](https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/the-disney-gallery-music-2-0.7840/#post-73222)  (2013) and Magic Music, [https://community.magicmusic.net/threads/disneyland-music-loops.17/page-2#post-218](https://community.magicmusic.net/threads/disneyland-music-loops.17/page-2#post-218)  (2002).
