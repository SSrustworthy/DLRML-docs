# [1987-1997] The Art of Disneyland v.2, [2001-2002] A Brush with Disney: The Art of Herbert Ryman

**Location**: New Orleans Square

**Loop Total Length**: Unknown

This loop played in the Disney Gallery in New Orleans Square. According to the Disneyland Forever Project PDF, "The Disney Gallery tracks are from a music loop first used in 1994.  The loop was removed for the New Tomorrowland exhibit but returned with the Herb Ryman exhibit in 2001 and 2002.  The music loop was probably first prepared for the Auberge de Cendrillon restaurant in Fantasyland at Disneyland Paris.” Also reported as “Disney Gallery Chateau Background Music” on Magic Music by Progressland in 2001, see [https://community.magicmusic.net/threads/disneyland-music-loops.17/post-72](https://community.magicmusic.net/threads/disneyland-music-loops.17/post-72)  (2002). Also see [https://disney-parks-music-compendium.blogspot.com/2022/11/the-disney-gallery-art-of-disneyland-bgm.html](https://disney-parks-music-compendium.blogspot.com/2022/11/the-disney-gallery-art-of-disneyland-bgm.html)  .
