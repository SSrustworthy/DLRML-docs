# [1997-2000] Tomorrowland: Looking at the Future 1955 - 1998

**Location**: New Orleans Square

**Loop Total Length**: ~00:38:57

This loop played at the Disney Gallery in New Orleans Square. In use for the duration of the Tomorrowland: Looking at the Future exhibit, from July 11, 1997 until 2000. Tracklist compiled by Progressland and wedroy1923. See Magic Music, [https://community.magicmusic.net/threads/disneyland-music-loops.17/post-72](https://community.magicmusic.net/threads/disneyland-music-loops.17/post-72)  (2002) and MouseBits, [https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/the-disney-gallery-music-2-0.7840/](https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/the-disney-gallery-music-2-0.7840/)  (2013). RocketRodsXPR noted that this loop “was for the largest portion of the gallery, although at least one of the rooms (the one featuring the 1998 models and art) had a different loop.” Also see MouseBits, [https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/the-disney-gallery.6886/](https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/the-disney-gallery.6886/)  (2011) and the Disney Parks Music Compendium tracklist [https://disney-parks-music-compendium.blogspot.com/2022/12/the-disney-gallery-tomorrowland.html](https://disney-parks-music-compendium.blogspot.com/2022/12/the-disney-gallery-tomorrowland.html)  .
