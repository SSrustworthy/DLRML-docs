# [1987-1997] The Art of Disneyland v.1

**Location**: New Orleans Square

**Loop Total Length**: Unknown

This loop played in the Disney Gallery in New Orleans Square, which opened with “The Art of Disneyland” exhibit on July 11, 1987. According to Magic Music user fairlight2x, tracks from the album “Disney Strings Fantasy” (1987, DL Records Japan) and/or Vol. 2 in the series were used as the gallery BGM prior to the late 90s. It is unclear which tracks were used. See [https://community.magicmusic.net/threads/disney-strings-fantasy.5071/](https://community.magicmusic.net/threads/disney-strings-fantasy.5071/)  (2015) and MouseBits, [https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/the-disney-gallery-music-2-0.7840/post-76673](https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/the-disney-gallery-music-2-0.7840/post-76673)  (2013).
