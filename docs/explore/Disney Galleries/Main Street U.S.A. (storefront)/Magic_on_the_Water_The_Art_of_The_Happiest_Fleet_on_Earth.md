# [2011] Magic on the Water – The Art of The Happiest Fleet on Earth

**Location**: Main Street U.S.A. (storefront)

**Loop Total Length**: ~00:23:52

This loop played in the Disney Gallery on Main Street, U.S.A. for the exhibit “Magic on the Water — The Art of The Happiest Fleet on Earth”, on display from January - June 2011. The exhibit highlighted the watercraft of Disney parks and Cruise Line. Playlist compiled by wedroy1923 based on two live recordings by pixelated and C33. See MouseBits, [https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/the-disney-gallery-music-2-0.7840/post-69938](https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/the-disney-gallery-music-2-0.7840/post-69938)  (2013), [https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/lotsa-new-audio-in-disney-galleryd-new-exhibit-1-29-11.6134/post-50019](https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/lotsa-new-audio-in-disney-galleryd-new-exhibit-1-29-11.6134/post-50019)  (2011) and[https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/the-disney-gallery-music-2-0.7840/post-69938](https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/the-disney-gallery-music-2-0.7840/post-69938)  (2013).
