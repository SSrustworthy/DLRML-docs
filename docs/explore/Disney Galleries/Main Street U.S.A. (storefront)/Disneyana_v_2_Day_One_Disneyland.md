# Disneyana v.2, [2010] Day One Disneyland

**Location**: Main Street U.S.A. (Disneyana), Main Street U.S.A. (storefront)

**Loop Total Length**: 00:33:55.353

This loop played in the Disney Gallery on Main Street, U.S.A. as part of the “Day One Disneyland” exhibit, which ran from May - December 2010. It was also used in the Main Street Cinema during the 55th celebration, and was noted as playing in Disneyana on MSUSA in 2023 and 2024 for no exhibit in particular. See MouseBits, [https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/disneyana-loop-on-main-street-u-s-a.10894/#post-99246](https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/disneyana-loop-on-main-street-u-s-a.10894/#post-99246)  (2023), [https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/the-disney-gallery-music-2-0.7840/#post-69937](https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/the-disney-gallery-music-2-0.7840/#post-69937)  (2013) and [https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/disneyana-loop-on-main-street-u-s-a.10894/](https://forums.mousebits.com/threads/disneyana-loop-on-main-street-u-s-a.10894/)  (2025).
